# Business Recommendations

Recommendations are prioritized by expected business impact and implementation horizon.

---

# Immediate Priorities

## 1. Roll Out Variant B

Variant B increased paid conversion from **17.4% to 20.2%** (+2.9 percentage points, +16% relative improvement).

The result was statistically significant (**p < 0.001**) with no evidence of negative impact on Revenue per User (ARPU).

Recommendation:

- Roll out Variant B to 100% of users.
- Continue monitoring post-launch retention and revenue guardrails.

---

## 2. Reallocate Marketing Spend

Referral and Organic acquisition channels consistently produced the highest paid conversion rates (~22–23%), while Paid Search converted at approximately 15%.

Recommendation:

- Increase investment in Referral and Organic acquisition.
- Re-evaluate Paid Search strategy and campaign quality.

---

# Medium-Term Priorities

## Improve Trial-to-Paid Conversion

The largest customer drop-off occurs between the trial and paid subscription stages.

Approximately 19,000 trial users fail to convert.

Recommendation:

- Improve onboarding.
- Increase early activation.
- Reduce friction during trial conversion.

---

## Focus on Product Experience Rather Than Pricing

Churn rates were nearly identical across Standard and Pro plans (~37%).

Recommendation:

- Prioritize activation and first-month engagement.
- Avoid pricing changes as the primary retention strategy.

---

## Monitor Early Engagement

Early user activity strongly influences long-term subscription outcomes.

Recommendation:

- Track activation metrics immediately after signup.
- Identify high-risk users before first renewal.

---

# Long-Term Priorities

## Build Predictive Models

Develop churn prediction models to identify high-risk customers before cancellation.

---

## Expand Experimentation

Continue controlled experimentation on:

- Onboarding
- Pricing
- Trial experience
- Product features

---

## Optimize Customer Lifetime Value

Shift decision-making toward long-term Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) rather than short-term conversion metrics alone.

---

These recommendations provide a prioritization framework based on the analytical findings presented in this project and should be validated continuously using production data and ongoing experimentation.
